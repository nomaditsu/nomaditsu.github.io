VersoCam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · Developer: Nomaditsu (Ray de Guzman)

The short version VersoCam does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app creates stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party tracking.

What data we collect

None. VersoCam does not gather any personal or usage data, and sends nothing to us or to any third party. The app has no servers and makes no network calls to transmit your information.

Camera and microphone

VersoCam uses your device's cameras and microphone only while you are recording, and only to produce the video you asked it to record. Recordings are written to your device's local storage / gallery. They are never uploaded, streamed, or transmitted off your device by VersoCam.

Photos and media access

The app requests access to your device's photos and video so it can save your recordings to your gallery and let you view them within the app. This access is used solely on your device.

Sharing your recordings

If you choose to share a recording using your device's standard share options, that action is handled by your device and the app or service you select — not by VersoCam.

Children's privacy

VersoCam is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
